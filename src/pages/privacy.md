This Privacy Policy explains how **PYROS AI Creative** collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you visit or use **www.pyrosaicreative.com** and related services.

For privacy-related questions or requests, you can contact:

**contact@pyrosaicreative.com**

## 1. Information Collected

The information collected depends on how you use the Website.

### Account Information

When you create a PYROS AI Creative account, information may be collected such as:

- Your email address
- Account authentication information
- Account creation date
- Information required to authenticate and manage your account

This information is used to create and maintain your account, authenticate access, provide account-only features, and protect the security of the Website.

Authentication and account data are processed using **Supabase**, which provides database, authentication, and storage infrastructure for the Website.

PYROS AI Creative does not receive or store your password in readable form.

## 2. Guide Downloads and Website Features

Some resources, including downloadable AI filmmaking guides, may require a registered account.

When you interact with these resources, information may be recorded such as:

- The guide or resource accessed
- The date and time of the interaction
- Your associated account identifier
- Technical information required to provide the download

This information may be used to operate the Website, understand which resources are useful to visitors, prevent abuse, and improve future content.

## 3. Newsletter and Marketing Emails

When creating an account or using other areas of the Website, you may be given the option to subscribe to the PYROS AI Creative newsletter.

Newsletter subscription is optional.

If you choose to subscribe, your email address may be used to send content such as:

- AI filmmaking tutorials
- New guides and resources
- PYROS AI Creative news and updates
- New videos or educational content
- Product announcements
- Offers or promotions

You may unsubscribe at any time using the unsubscribe link included in marketing emails.

Unsubscribing from marketing emails does not delete your PYROS AI Creative account and does not prevent essential account, security, or service-related messages from being sent when necessary.

Your newsletter preference may be stored so that your choice can be respected.

When you visit the Website, certain information may be collected automatically.

This may include:

- IP address or approximate geographic location

- Pages visited

## 5. Google Analytics

PYROS AI Creative uses, or may use, **Google Analytics 4 ("Google Analytics")** to understand how visitors interact with the Website.

Google Analytics may collect information such as:

- Page views
- Sessions
- Approximate location
- Device and browser information
